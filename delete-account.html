Delete your InkTasks account

In the app: Settings → Account and subscription → Delete account.

Without the app: email support@inktasks.com from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and its subscription record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What's deleted: your name, email, account identifier and subscription record. What isn't: your notes, which were never on our servers and stay on your device. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel it there first.
