InkTasks privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

InkTasks is a handwriting notes app made by Ahmed Al Balushi ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your notes stay on your device. We never see them, upload them, sell them or use them for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, handwriting, pictures, PDFs, folders and tasks. They are stored only on your device. Handwriting is recognised on the device; it is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Your calendar. If you turn on calendar sync, InkTasks adds your tasks as events to a calendar on your device that you choose, and updates or removes only the events it created. It doesn't read your other events. If that calendar is one your device syncs with an online account (such as Google Calendar), that account's own provider syncs it, as it does for any event you add.
  • Pictures and files you add. A picture or PDF you pick is copied into the app's storage on your device. The app sees only the files you pick.
  • Reminders and the daily summary. These are scheduled and shown on your device.
  • Backups. A backup is a single file saved where you choose with your device's file picker — on the device, or in a cloud storage service you pick (such as Google Drive). It goes only there; we never receive a copy. Whoever provides that storage handles the file under their own terms.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Your account — To subscribe, you sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your name, email address and an account identifier from them, through Firebase Authentication (a Google service). This is so one subscription works on all your devices. If you choose Apple's "Hide my email", we receive only a private relay address.
  • Your subscription — Purchases are made through Google Play (or the App Store on iPad). We use RevenueCat to confirm whether your account has an active subscription. RevenueCat receives your account identifier and your purchase history for InkTasks (product, dates, renewal status) — not your payment card details, which stay with Google or Apple.
  • Crash reports — If the app crashes, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics: what went wrong in the code, your device model, Android version, app version and an identifier for this installation. Crash reports never include your notes.
  • Handwriting models — The first time you use a recognition language, the app downloads that language's model from Google. Google's ML Kit, which does the recognition on your device, also sends Google basic usage and performance information (such as device model, app version and error codes) to keep the service working. It never sends your handwriting.

Everything sent is encrypted in transit. We don't use advertising, ad identifiers or tracking across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or rent any information.

Who processes it for us

Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Crashlytics, ML Kit, Google Play), Apple (Sign in with Apple, the App Store) and RevenueCat process the information above on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

How long we keep it

  • Your account information and subscription record: until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • If the law requires us to keep a record of a purchase for longer (for example, for tax), we keep only that record, for as long as required.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account and subscription → Delete account. Or on the web, at https://inktasks.com/delete-account, or by writing to support@inktasks.com from the email address on your account. Deleting your account removes it, and the subscription record linked to it, from our services within 30 days. Your notes are not affected — they were never on our servers and stay on your device. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play (or your Apple ID's settings) first, or it keeps renewing.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device, notes included, unless you saved a backup first.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how it's used. Write to support@inktasks.com and we'll answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

InkTasks is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to support@inktasks.com and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, say so in the app before they take effect.
